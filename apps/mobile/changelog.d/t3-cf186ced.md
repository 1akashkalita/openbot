### Changed

- Touch and hold a message to see all its actions. The chat dims, and the message rises a little, with reactions above it and Reply and Copy under it, on Liquid Glass on iOS. Before, a touch and hold only selected text in a reply.
- To use part of a message, select it in the held message. Then you can copy it, use Explain, Improve, Shorten, Tone or Grammar, or choose Describe edits to write your own change. On Android, tap Select text first.
- The Explain, Improve, Shorten, Tone and Grammar buttons above the message field are gone. They are now in the held message.
