### Added

- The website has a Pricing page. It shows the hosted servers at their Stripe prices, the Starter
  license (1 member) and the Team license (5 to 25 members), monthly or yearly, in EUR, USD or PLN.
  Choose a server, a license or both. The summary shows the total. Visitors in the EU see 23% VAT
  in the summary. When the Stripe prices do not load, the page shows the list prices.
- The header and the footer of the website have a Pricing link.
- The Pricing page button opens the web app at the add server dialog or at Billing.
