### Changed

- Sell the commercial license in two plans. Starter is €30 a month for 1 user. Team is €100 a month
  for 5 users and €15 for each extra user, up to 25. Yearly billing takes 10% off. An account that
  pays for a hosted server (any plan) pays €10 less a month for Starter and €20 less for Team. Each
  user can use up to 3 devices.
- Billing in the app has the same order form as the Pricing page. Order a server and a license
  together with one payment.
- Change a Starter license to Team, or a monthly Team license to yearly, from Billing. When an
  invite needs more than 1 user, the members section offers the change to Team.
- Allow 1 member on a new Starter server. Starter servers that already exist keep 3 members.
